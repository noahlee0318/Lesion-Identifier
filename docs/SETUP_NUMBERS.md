# Setup numbers — the rig, written down

Fill this in **as you build the rig**, not afterwards from memory.

The rig will get knocked over, the lamp will get borrowed, someone will move
the tape. When that happens this page is the difference between rebuilding it
in five minutes and quietly shooting at a different geometry for three weeks
without noticing.

Lighting drift and scale drift are both invisible until month three, when the
chart shows a step change on the day something moved.
