How to upload from your phone

Shoot with the normal Camera app, then come back here and attach the photos. This page never opens the camera itself.
  1. Camera app → take the shots for this session. Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible so it writes JPEG, not HEIC.
  2. Come back to this page and tap Choose File under each pose.
  3. Pick Photo Library and select that pose's shot. iOS converts HEIC to JPEG on the way out, which is why Most Compatible matters — it keeps the conversion out of the loop entirely.
  4. Set the lens you used, answer the tap rows, hit Upload.
  5. Read the verdict. If a pose says RESHOOT, it tells you what was wrong and what to change — do it now, while the lamp and the tape are still where they were.
It says server unreachable
The laptop is asleep, off, or on a different Wi-Fi. Nothing is lost — the photos sit in your camera roll and upload whenever you get back to this page. Uploading hours late costs nothing; the session date field is what dates the data, not the upload time.
Keep Originals, and why the file must not be re-encoded
Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, and Settings → Photos → Transfer to Mac or PC → Keep Originals. The server stores exactly the bytes it receives — no resize, no EXIF strip. Run scripts/verify_upload_fidelity.py once before the first real session to confirm Safari is not altering the file.
